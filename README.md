## Building Tuliya

I'm currently building **Tuliya** from the ground up as a solo founder.

The idea is simple: make it easier to discover useful AI tools, software, resources and curated collections — and make comparing them easier too.

I'm working across the entire product, from the interface and backend to search, SEO, content systems, database architecture and deployment.

It's still a work in progress. I'm building it, learning from it, and improving it one piece at a time.

🌐 **[infotic.in](https://www.infotic.in)**

---

## A bit about me

I'm a backend-focused developer who enjoys going beyond just making things work.

I like understanding what's happening underneath — how APIs are designed, how databases behave under load, where caching helps, how services communicate, and how everything eventually gets deployed.

Most of my current learning happens through building real systems and experimenting with them.
